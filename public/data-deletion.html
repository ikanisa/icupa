To request deletion of an ICUPA account or personal data, send an email to info@ikanisa.com with the subject ICUPA data deletion request.

Explain whether you want to delete your account, request deletion of specific personal data, or ask what data is held. Provide enough information to help us find the correct account, such as your ICUPA account email or the phone number used with the service.

Security: Do not email passwords, WhatsApp verification codes, access tokens, identity-document scans, payment credentials or other sensitive secrets. We may need to verify account ownership through a separate secure method before taking action.

IKANISA LTD will review the request and explain the next steps, including whether any specific data must be retained to meet applicable legal or security obligations. Submission of a request is not confirmation that deletion has been completed.

For other privacy questions, contact info@ikanisa.com.

This page provides a request channel only. Actual backend deletion, provider-data processing, retention and fulfilment need separate operational verification before this page is supplied to Meta as a complete deletion process.
